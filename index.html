Understand the field

People. Ideas.
Measurable impact.

Marketing brings together creativity, research and business strategy. You learn to understand customer needs, explain the value of products and services, and build meaningful connections.

Those skills can support a new business, a nonprofit campaign, a sports organization or a technology company.

You may already think like a marketer.

Have you made social content, designed a logo, organized an event, written a blog, produced a video or helped promote a project? Those experiences give you a starting point for developing marketing skills.

Understand people

Explore consumer psychology and why people make choices.

Tell a clear story

Communicate ideas through writing, presentations and content.

Find the insight

Use data and market research to inform decisions.

Build a brand

Connect positioning, visual identity and customer experience.

Think strategically

Develop solutions to business problems and evaluate results.

Work with technology

Explore digital media, marketing tools and emerging AI applications.

Questions students ask

Can I study online?
The program page lists campus, online and mixed study options. Confirm your schedule and course availability with an advisor.
What GPA and credits do I need?
The 2026–27 degree plan lists a minimum 2.00 Missouri State GPA and 2.50 combined GPA for College of Business admission and upper-division business courses. Graduation requires at least 120 credits, including 40 upper-division credits, and a 2.50 GPA in upper-division College of Business business-unit coursework taken at Missouri State. Review the official plan and confirm all admission and graduation requirements with an advisor.
I'm transferring. Where do I start?
Work with the Business Advisement Center to review credits and course sequencing before choosing a schedule. Visit business advising.
Does the degree guarantee a particular job or salary?
No. Career paths depend on your skills, experience and the job market. Use occupation data alongside local job listings, internships and advising to set realistic goals.
Where can I find costs and scholarships?
University costs and department scholarships and financial aid are the starting points for planning your budget.
Where are faculty and official program resources?
Visit the Marketing Department for faculty, courses, student learning outcomes and contact information.
